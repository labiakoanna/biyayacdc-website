Meet the children awaiting a sponsor

Every profile below follows our published eligibility criteria and dignity standards. Full names and photographs go live only once local staff complete registration and guardian consent.

Beneficiary eligibility criteria

Who we support

Age range

Children aged 3 to 17 years old, with priority given to ages 3–9.

Geographic bound

Long-term residents within a 3 km radius of the Centre in Biyaya Village.

Vulnerability level

Orphans, the chronically ill lacking family medical cover, or the neediest of the needy.

Children already receiving support from another active sponsorship agency are not eligible, to avoid duplication of care.

Registering a new child

Local staff complete registration and consent through our internal portal before any profile is published, in line with Article 8 of our constitution.

  • Full legal name, date of birth, village & parish residence
  • Vulnerability assessment: orphan status, chronic illness, lack of relative support
  • Signed parent/guardian consent and Child Protection Agreement on file
  • Verified 3 km geographic radius compliance
