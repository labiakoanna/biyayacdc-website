Life at Biyaya CDC

A glimpse into our children’s everyday growth

From life-skills workshops to music and celebration, these moments show our five core pillars in action — captured with dignity, and shared with the consent of our caregivers.

Life skills & talent development

Baking, music and everyday joy

Every activity below ties back to our constitutional pillars — life skills education, moral & spiritual development, and holistic mentorship — carried out through hands-on, age-appropriate programming.

Life Skills

Baking & Pastry Workshop

Children take turns measuring, mixing coloured icing and preparing batter together — a hands-on life-skills session that builds practical ability, teamwork and patience.

Celebration

Celebrating Together

The boys proudly present a cake iced by their peers. Birthdays and milestones at the Centre are marked as a family — nurturing belonging and self-worth.

Music & Talent

Marching Band Practice

Our children's brass and drum band rehearses discipline and rhythm together — a talent-development activity that builds confidence and stage presence.

Nutrition

Fresh From the Oven

The reward for a morning of teamwork: freshly baked treats shared alongside a nutritious plate of posho and stew, reinforcing our health & nutrition pillar.

Nutrition

Tasting Time

Laughter and taste-testing follow every workshop — a simple, joyful moment that caps off the morning's baking activity.

Nutrition

Sharing the Harvest

Once the baking is done, everyone shares in the results together — a small but meaningful lesson in gratitude and community.

Educational Sponsorship

Back-to-School Provision

A new school uniform, exercise books and personal care items — practical educational sponsorship support that keeps a child confident and ready to learn.

Music & Talent

Full Band Rehearsal

Trumpets, trombones and drums come together under the mango trees — a growing tradition that gives our children a shared sense of pride and discipline.

Music & Talent

Rhythm Section at Work

Consistent practice on the drum line builds focus and teamwork — skills that carry well beyond music into everyday life.

Health Campaigns

Growth Monitoring & Health Outreach

Our health team weighs and screens infants in the community, catching early signs of malnutrition or illness — a core part of our health campaigns pillar.

Life Skills

Champions on the Field

Rival teams pose together with their medals and trophies after a friendly tournament — football builds discipline, teamwork and healthy competition.

Educational Sponsorship

Proud in Our Uniforms

A shared laugh after school — every uniform here represents a sponsorship that keeps a child enrolled, confident and belonging.

Moral & Spiritual Development

Home Visit & Bible Study

Beyond the Centre, our homebased program team visits families for scripture reading and pastoral care — carrying our mission of discipleship into the home.

Celebration

Cutting the Cake Together

Many hands on one knife — every celebration at Biyaya CDC is a shared one, marked as one big family.

Cognitive Development

Early Learning Together

In our cognitive development corner, our donor relations & cognitive development officer guides a child's very first pencil strokes — building fine motor skills early.

Cognitive Development

Little Hands, Big Steps

Every stroke on paper is a milestone — patient, one-on-one attention helps our youngest children build the foundations for school readiness.

Educational Sponsorship

Hands Up to Learn

A full house for our literacy class — eager hands raised to sound out letters and words together in our open-air classroom.

Educational Sponsorship

Reading Vocabulary at the Board

One pupil takes a turn sounding out new words at the board — building the reading confidence that carries into formal schooling.

Economic Empowerment

Family Food Distribution

Sacks of maize and beans, and cartons of milk, packed and ready for caregiver families — practical support that eases the burden at home.

Economic Empowerment

Provisions for the Season

Seasonal food support strengthens the households behind every child we serve, easing pressure on caregivers doing their best to provide.

Health Campaigns

Malnutrition Screening

A simple arm-circumference check can catch early signs of malnutrition — our outreach team screens infants right in their own neighbourhoods.

Health Campaigns

Community Health Follow-up

Every measurement is logged on the child's health card, so growth can be tracked visit after visit and concerns caught early.

Moral & Spiritual Development

Checking In at Home

Our homebased team sits with a grandmother and her grandchildren, sharing materials and encouragement — care that follows children beyond our gates.

Life Skills

Guest Speaker Session

A visiting facilitator leads a life skills talk, encouraging children toward education and empowerment beyond the classroom.

Life Skills

Community Sensitization Assembly

Hundreds of children gather under the mango trees for a community-wide awareness session — reaching far beyond our own compound.

Economic Empowerment

Vocational Tailoring Lessons

Caregivers and youth learn the fundamentals of tailoring — a practical trade skill that builds toward sustainable household income.

Economic Empowerment

Hands-On Sewing Practice

From chalkboard theory straight to the machine — trainees put new tailoring skills into practice under their instructor's eye.

Economic Empowerment

Sewing Machines for Self-Reliance

Graduating trainees receive sewing machines of their own — equipping caregiver mothers to earn an income long after the training ends.

Economic Empowerment

Learning to Sew Together

Mothers bring their little ones along and take turns at the machines — a trade skill built one stitch, and one shared morning, at a time.

Economic Empowerment

A Family's Monthly Provisions

Eggs, maize flour, beans and milk — a household's share of support, collected and ready to carry home.

Economic Empowerment

Ready for the Week Ahead

Behind every bag of provisions is a family we walk with — practical support that eases the load at home.

Health Campaigns

Mothers' Health Talk

Mothers gather in the shade with their babies for a health and parenting talk — practical guidance shared community to community.

Health Campaigns

Hygiene Kits for Mothers

Soap and hygiene supplies handed out at the close of our health talk — simple tools that protect family health at home.

Health Campaigns

Celebrating Clean Homes

All smiles as mothers head home with hygiene kits in hand — a small but joyful outcome of a morning well spent together.

Cognitive Development

Our Cognitive Development Learning Centre

A dedicated space built for early learning, painted with scenes of the very children it serves — reading, resting and playing together.

Economic Empowerment

Turning Fabric into Products

From lessons to livelihood — trainees put their new skills to work, sewing patterned fabric goods they can sell in their own community.
