14 August 2026 · Cognitive Development

Our Cognitive Development Learning Centre Is Open

We're delighted to share that our dedicated Cognitive Development Learning Centre is complete and already in use. Painted with scenes of children reading, resting and playing, the space gives our youngest children a proper home for early-learning sessions — guided pencil practice, storytelling and play-based development — led by our Donor Relations & Cognitive Development officer, Giramia Jane. Thank you to every partner whose giving made this building possible.
